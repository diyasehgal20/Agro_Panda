ABOUT 

Indian agriculture is beset by a number of issues that either directly or indirectly affect a farmer's life, whether we talk about the issue of underemployment or unemployment that farmers must endure for a significant portion of the year due to a lack of work on the farms and the absence of alternative sources of employment, or we talk about the issues that female agricultural workers must deal with while still being paid less than what men receive.In addition, the inadequate state of farm equipment makes life difficult for farmers.

Our one step platform, where all sorts of farmers are treated fairly, where there is never a period of the year without work opportunities, and a shortage of agricultural tools, aims to address all these issues.
